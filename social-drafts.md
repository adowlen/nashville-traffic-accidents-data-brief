## LinkedIn draft (company page, for a future Friday synthesis)

30.2% of the people killed in Nashville reported crashes were pedestrians.

Metro's traffic accidents layer holds 222,903 reported crashes from January 2019 through October 2026. Pedestrians were involved in 1.5% of them - and in 280 of the 926 deaths.

The second pattern is severity per reported crash by lighting: 1.14% of reported crashes coded dark-not-lighted were fatal crashes, against 0.22% in daylight. That is per reported crash, not per mile driven - the layer has no traffic-volume denominator, and the brief prints that limit with the figure. Hit-and-run is 27.1% of reported crashes with the field populated.
